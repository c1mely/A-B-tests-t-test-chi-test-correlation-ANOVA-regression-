# Education, Religiosity, and Life Satisfaction in Italy

## Overview
This project explores how education level and religiosity are associated 
with life satisfaction among Italian respondents, using data from the 
European Social Survey Round 11 (2023)

## Research Question
To what extent do education and religiosity predict life satisfaction 
in Italy, and does education level moderate the relationship between 
religiosity and well-being?

## Methods
- Independent samples t-test
- Chi-square test of independence
- Spearman correlation
- One-way ANOVA (Games-Howell)
- Linear regression with model comparison
- Interaction analysis (education × religiosity)

## Data
European Social Survey (ESS) Round 11, Italy subsample (n = 2,682).  
Data available at: https://www.europeansocialsurvey.org

## Tools
R, dplyr, tidyverse, car, rstatix

## Key Findings
- Education is a significant positive predictor of life satisfaction
- Religiosity shows a small but significant effect when age is controlled for
- Education does not moderate the effect of religiosity on life satisfaction
